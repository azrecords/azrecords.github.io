is there any music equipment (anything like VSTs or sample packs) you find essential?
</div>

<div class="interviewee">

<div class="interviewee-answer">
Blu Mar Ten jungle jungle, AMG, nastynasty, jungle warfare are my go to amen break sample packs that i use for just about everything! if you're in the dnb space then these are super essential imo (and the majority of producers definitely have one or more of them)

<br><br>
is there something that makes a track "release-worthy" to you; a quality that marks something as a stand-out among the sketches? how much do you concern yourself with improvement relative to your previous record(s)?
</div>

<div class="interviewee">
<font size="5" color="#6358cc">prayboymantis</font> <font color="#AAAAAA">2026-11-06T20:40:34.318Z</font><br>
</div>
<div class="interviewee-answer">
i release tracks regularly based on if the finish work reflects something that im feeling in the moment, or something that can relate to an emotion im experiencing at the time.
<br><br>
my song titling can be very vulnerable in my "diary entry" style of naming songs, ill sift through projects named either a random string of letters or "project 110" until i can feel me wanting to give it a name that speaks to how im connecting with it - thats the cue to me that its worth releasing :)
</div>
<div class="interviewee"><font color="#AAAAAA">2026-11-06T20:44:09.869Z</font><br>
</div>
<div class="interviewee-answer">
i try not to concern myself too much on making each song better than the last or one upping myself, all of my works are messy in their own right and i feel like that adds to my music journey the more i create! however when it comes to more technical things i do TRY to remember todo certain stuff, for the sake of flow and making it not burst anyone's ear drums
</div>

<br><br>
how do you perceive the importance of releasing an album, especially for an artist's growth? simply put - why <i>not</i> just put out 8 singles?
do you think you'll work on more large projects like that?
</div>

<div class="interviewee">
<font size="5" color="#6358cc">prayboymantis</font> <font color="#AAAAAA">2026-11-06T21:19:26.170Z</font><br>
</div>
<div class="interviewee-answer">
i think if you're a small artist like myself, creating an album or a big project is super important to your credibility and mentality as a growing artist. BUT from a pure "feed the machine" mentality, it just makes sense to span out consistent releases so you can reach more algorithms and audiences with dedicated single releases.
</div>
<div class="interviewee"><font color="#AAAAAA">2026-11-06T21:23:15.331Z</font><br>
</div>
<div class="interviewee-answer">
its actually really annoying having to dance between the love of pure creation and then the more dull and bland brush of modern day marketing and "strategies" to promote yourself and your works.
</div>
<div class="interviewee"><font color="#AAAAAA">2026-11-06T21:25:05.730Z</font><br>
</div>
<div class="interviewee-answer">
as for my own projects in the future, ive been working on a few in the background of my single releases! im hoping this time i can right a few wrongs i did with my previous album, and also have a more polished and better flow feel throughout it :)
</div>
